6-Lever Hierarchy Playbook (inside the 4-size ceiling)

ds-typography-levers
src/styles/tokens.css & src/index.css
1 · Weight polarity (400 / 500 / 600 / 700)

700 Bold is locked strictly to 32px Large Titles and Hero KPIs. 600 Semibold anchors 20px card/nav headers, inputs & Primary CTAs. 500 Medium handles list rows (.wk-subhead), secondary buttons, fields, eyebrows, micro titles & pills. 400 Regular keeps prose, units, dates, ticks & inactive tabs airy.

2 · Ink opacity & WCAG AA contrast

#FFFFFF (1.00, 13.30:1 card) for titles & hero numbers; rgba 0.78 (8.74:1 card / 7.13:1 raised / 5.67:1 ctrl) for prose, date steppers & pills; rgba 0.60 (5.86:1 card / 4.96:1 raised) for eyebrows, units, dates & inactive controls.

3 · Case & tracking discipline

Sentence case is the default everywhere (titles, goals, Trophy lifts, pills, weekday Sun..Sat, dates Oct 17 – Nov 15, 2026). Uppercase (+0.06em) is strictly quarantined to 1–3 word eyebrows and column headers (.wk-label).

4 · Navigation vs card differentiation

Main tabs anchor top-left at 32px/700 (-0.03em Large Title), separating pages cleanly from centered 20px/600 drill-in DetailHeader (.wk-nav-title) and 20px/600 left card headers (.wk-title).

5 · Tracking matched to scale

Tight display tracking (-0.03em at 32px, -0.02em at 20px, -0.015em on tabular numbers) gives large headlines Apple tight solidity while +0.06em opens small uppercase eyebrows and +0.005em eases 12px captions.

6 · Saturation restraint

Mint (--color-status-positive) and Info Blue (--color-status-info) live strictly in chart strokes, deltas, completion checks & active streaks — never decorative card text or shouting section banners.
